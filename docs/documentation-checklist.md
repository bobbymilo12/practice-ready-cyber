# COMP3000 Documentation Checklist

Taken from the COMP3000 Assignment Brief 26/27. Everything goes on the PebblePad ePortfolio unless stated otherwise. All deadlines close at 15:00.

## 01 Supervisor Selection (deadline 12 Oct 2026)
- [ ] Sign up to your supervisor's group on the DLE
- [ ] Fill in ePortfolio section "01. Supervisor Selection"

## 02 Project Initiation / PID (deadline 9 Nov 2026)
- [ ] Project title (must reflect your degree, so a security topic)
- [ ] Keywords
- [ ] Project vision (also put the title, vision and supervisor in the repo `README.md`)
- [ ] Literature review
- [ ] Risk assessment covering likelihood and impact (any high-likelihood, high-impact risk must have mitigating actions)
- [ ] Gantt chart
- [ ] Initial product backlog, plus your tech stack choice, development environment and test environment (sprint 0 outputs)
- [ ] Give your supervisor access to the GitHub repo (the repo name should reflect your name)

## Ongoing all year
- [ ] **Sprint diary**: one entry per 2-week sprint, as bullet points. Each entry needs:
  - the sprint dates
  - the supervisor meeting date
  - the work completed
  - the supervisor's feedback and actions
  - the plan for the next sprint
- [ ] **3 CPD reflections** of no more than 250 words each (reflect on what you learned, don't just describe the activity)
- [ ] **Planner and diary** for managing your time (recommended by the brief, and they count towards the planning marks)

## 03 Poster & Description
- [ ] **Draft for feedback, 8 Mar 2027.** Optional, but it's your progress checkpoint.
- [ ] **Print-ready version, 5 Apr 2027.** Mandatory.
  - A1 landscape `.jpg` named `<StudentID>.jpg`
  - A thumbnail of about 100 KB named `<StudentID>-thumb.jpg`
- [ ] **Final title, final description and updated keywords**
- [ ] **Poster content:** the problem, your solution, methodology, results, and app screenshots

## Showcase (6 May 2027, 09:00–16:00)
- [ ] Attend. Attendance is mandatory and counts towards the practice mark.

## 05 Viva (week of 10 May 2027)
- [ ] A 10-minute presentation, then questions, about 30 minutes in total
- [ ] Go deeper than the video rather than repeating it
- [ ] Attend. Missing the viva fails the whole module.

## Report sections, based on the marking criteria

| Section | Weight | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Project definition & planning | 10% | SMART aims and objectives; a plan that you update as you go; sprint plans that map to the backlog |
| Context review | 15% | Critical literature review; correct citations; legal, social, ethical and professional (LSEP) issues such as UK GDPR and NHS data protection |
| Methodology & implementation | 50% | Justified methodology with alternatives considered; evidence of agile artifacts (backlog, sprints, reviews); architecture; principles like DRY, YAGNI and SOLID; explanation of any deviation from the plan |
| Evaluation & conclusions | 15% | Test plan with verification and validation; results; critical evaluation; conclusions tied to your aims; further work |
| Structure & presentation | 10% | Logical structure; clear figures with labels |

### Placeholder data
The platform uses only placeholder data: fictional patients, callers and practice details. The report should:
- Explain why only placeholder data is used. This counts towards the LSEP marks.
- Base the evaluation on automated tests and walkthroughs of the scenarios instead of user studies.
