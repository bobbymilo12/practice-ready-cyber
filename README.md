# PracticeReady Cyber: Scenario-Based Social Engineering and Data Protection Training for GP Reception Staff

**Module:** COMP3000 Computing Project (2026/27)
**Supervisor:** Dr Muhammad Asad

## Project Vision

GP reception and admin teams are the first point of contact for patients, and the first target for social engineering. Callers posing as patients, relatives or other professionals can try to pull confidential health data out of busy staff, who rarely get realistic practice at spotting and handling these requests.

PracticeReady Cyber is a web-based training platform. Reception staff work through realistic, scenario-based phone and front-desk interactions. These scenarios mix routine requests with social engineering attempts such as pretexting, impersonation and urgency pressure. Staff build the habits needed to verify identity, protect patient data under UK GDPR, and follow their practice's local procedures. Practice managers can set up modules that match their own procedures, assign them to trainees and track results over time. The aim is measurably better security behaviour at the human layer of primary care.
